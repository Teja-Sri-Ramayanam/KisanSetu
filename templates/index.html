The Problem

Physical Overcrowding & Uncertainty

Farmers waste many hours waiting in physical queues at procurement centers with loaded tractor trolleys under the harsh sun, suffering delays, traffic jams, and uncertainty about grain intake.

  • Unpredictable 8–14 hour physical queues at mandis.
  • Middlemen exploitation and lack of transparent scheduling.
  • Delay in payment disbursement and crop weight inspection.

The Kisan Setu Solution

Smart Slot Booking & Digital Tokens

Book a time slot, receive a digital token, and track procurement status online. Arrive only when your turn comes, experience fast-track intake, and receive direct payments seamlessly.

  • Pre-booked 2-hour procurement time slots with capacity limits.
  • QR-coded Digital Token with live queue tracking (5 min/person estimate).
  • Direct digital status synchronization with Officer and Admin portals.
